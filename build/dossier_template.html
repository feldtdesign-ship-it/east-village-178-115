Four minutes on foot. Down 11th, across to Avenue A, south along the park's edge. The camera is walking it now. The meter at the bottom right counts the distance as you scroll.

This is the whole argument for doing the two doors together. One neighbourhood, one park, one L station, two frontages that see different crowds.

The L at 1st Avenue

About 20,800 people enter this station on a summer weekday, and about the same come out. At 5 pm that is 3,400 an hour through the stairs at 14th Street, 276 m from one door and 536 m from the other. Everything else that arrives by transit near these doors is small.
